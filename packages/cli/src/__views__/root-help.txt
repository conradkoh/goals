Usage: goals <command> [options]

Command-line access to your Goals app data.

Commands:
  auth        Log in, log out and manage identities.
  goal        Read and change your goals.
  initiative  Read your initiatives.
  export      Export your goals as markdown.

Global options:
  --help, -h  Show help for a command.
  --version   Print the installed version.

Getting started:
  goals auth login
  goals auth status
  goals <command> --help
