Usage: goals goal <action> [options]

Read and change your goals.

Actions:
  list      List goals in one scope for a year (quarterly by default).
  get       Show one goal with its parent, children, details and due date.
  create    Create one quarterly, weekly, daily or adhoc goal.
  rename    Rename one goal.
  complete  Mark one goal complete.
  reopen    Mark one goal incomplete again.
  delete    Delete one goal and all of its child goals.

Run `goals goal <action> --help` for details.
