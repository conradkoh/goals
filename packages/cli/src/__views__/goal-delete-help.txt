Usage: goals goal delete <id> --yes [--as <label>]

Delete one goal and all of its child goals.

Options:
  <id>          Goal ID from `goals goal list` (use --json for scripts).
  --yes         Confirm the deletion; nothing is sent without it.
  --as <label>  Identity to use (default: the default identity).

Examples:
  goals goal delete goal_123 --yes

Notes:
  Deletes this goal and all of its child goals.
  --yes is required and nothing is sent without it.
  Goal log history is kept.
  IDs come from `goals goal list` (use --json for scripts).
